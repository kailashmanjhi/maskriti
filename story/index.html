Why Maskriti Exists

The Maskriti Story

Maskriti began with a simple belief: a mask can carry more than ornament. It can hold memory, performance vocabulary, material intelligence, and a distinct sense of presence.

We create collectible Indian mask forms for people who value objects with cultural depth, visible handwork, and stories that continue beyond the moment of making.

Myth, Memory & Form

Myth, memory, and form

Myth enters Maskriti quietly: through expressive eyes, familiar silhouettes, protective gestures, and forms that feel remembered even when encountered for the first time.

Myth

Context

Chhau Inspiration

Chhau inspiration and cultural respect

Maskriti draws from the visual grammar associated with Purulia Chhau: heightened eyes, sculptural crowns, rhythmic symmetry, and painted expressions built to read from a distance.

That vocabulary is approached through study and respect. Our pieces are collectible interpretations for contemporary placement, not replacements for masks made for performance.

Material Presence

Handmade objects for contemporary spaces

Papier-mâché structure, layered pigment, matte surfaces, and subtle asymmetry give each Maskriti piece a tactile presence that machine-perfect objects cannot reproduce.

The result is designed to live comfortably in considered interiors, studios, collector displays, and cultural spaces without losing the evidence of the hand.
