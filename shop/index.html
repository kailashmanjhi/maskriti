Shop FAQ

Are Maskriti masks handmade?

Yes. Each piece is built and hand-finished in small batches, so subtle variations are part of the craft.

Are these performance masks or wall decor pieces?

Maskriti pieces are collectible display objects inspired by Chhau visual grammar; they are not intended for performance use.

Can I request a custom mask?

Yes. Custom enquiries can be shaped around a chosen story, mood, form, or cultural space.

How do I order?

Use the WhatsApp enquiry button on a product to confirm availability, timelines, and delivery details.

Do you ship across India?

Shipping across India is available, subject to destination and protective packing requirements.
