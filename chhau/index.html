Living Performance Tradition

What is Chhau?

Chhau is a traditional dance-drama form from eastern India, associated especially with regions of West Bengal, Jharkhand, and Odisha. Its performance language combines vigorous movement, martial energy, music, mythology, storytelling, and stylized character expression. Chhau dance is listed by UNESCO on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

Reference: UNESCO — Chhau Dance, Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage.

Regional Craft Identity

Purulia Chhau and the Mask Tradition

Among Chhau traditions, Purulia Chhau is especially known for its elaborate masks and theatrical headgear. These masks are closely associated with mythological characters and a highly expressive visual style. The regional craft is officially listed as “Purulia Chau Mask” under GI Application No. 565. The Government of West Bengal's GI products document also lists Purulia Chau Mask with registration year 2018.

Our Position

Maskriti does not claim to replace or reproduce performance masks. Our work is Chhau-inspired: it studies visual cues, craft presence, and mythic form, then reinterprets them as contemporary collectible wall objects.

References: IP India GI Registry — Purulia Chau Mask, Application No. 565; Government of West Bengal — GI Products of West Bengal: Purulia Chau Mask.

Questions

Chhau Masks FAQ

What is a Chhau mask?

A Chhau mask is a highly expressive mask associated with Chhau dance traditions, especially Purulia Chhau. It helps define mythological, heroic, divine, animal, or symbolic characters through face shape, colour, crown, and expression.

Are Maskriti masks used for Chhau performance?

No. Maskriti pieces are Chhau-inspired collectible wall objects. They are made for interiors, collectors, gifting, and cultural spaces, not for stage performance.

What is Purulia Chhau known for?

Purulia Chhau is known for vigorous dance movement, mythological storytelling, elaborate masks, dramatic crowns, and strong visual character expression.

Can Chhau-inspired masks be used as wall decor?

Yes. A Chhau-inspired mask can become a powerful wall object when placed with space around it, especially in entrances, living rooms, studios, meditation rooms, and cultural corners.

Does Maskriti make custom Chhau-inspired pieces?

Yes. Custom pieces can be created around a deity, story, mood, room, or gifting occasion. Enquiries can be shared through the contact page or WhatsApp.

Are all Maskriti pieces handmade?

Yes. Each piece is handmade in small batches, so slight variations in form, finish, pigment, and surface character are part of the craft.
