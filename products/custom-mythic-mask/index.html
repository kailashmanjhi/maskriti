The Commission

A story translated into form

A Custom Mythic Mask begins with context: the story, archetype, mood, palette, scale, and placement you have in mind. These references guide the silhouette, painted expression, and level of detail.

The process is collaborative but remains grounded in Maskriti's material language: papier-mâché logic, tactile finish, and an object designed to carry presence.

  • Hand-painted pigment layers
