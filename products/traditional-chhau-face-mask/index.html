The Piece

Expressive form, held in stillness

This mask studies the heightened gaze, crown geometry, painted symmetry, and dramatic facial language associated with Chhau. The form is translated into a collectible object intended to be read slowly at close range.

Clay-grain texture and hand-painted detail preserve a sense of performance energy without presenting the piece as a functional stage mask.
