The Piece

A form of welcome and presence

The Ganesha Mukha Mask brings together an elephant-headed silhouette, layered crown detail, warm pigment, and a calm frontal gaze. It is conceived as a collectible object rather than a mass-produced symbol.

Visible handwork and subtle asymmetry allow the piece to hold its own character while remaining composed enough for contemporary placement.

Materials

  • Hand-shaped papier-mâché base
  • Natural pigment-inspired colour
  • Layered crown detailing
  • Matte protective finish

Use Cases

  • Studio and collector walls
  • Meditation and reading spaces
  • Meaningful cultural gifting

Made by Hand
