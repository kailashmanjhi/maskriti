The Craft Story

Made by hand. Shaped by tradition.

Maskriti celebrates the handmade quality of Indian mask-making: visible paper grain, subtle asymmetry, matte finishes, and forms that feel human rather than machine-perfect.

  • 01Form MouldingBuilt with patient shaping and tactile form work.
